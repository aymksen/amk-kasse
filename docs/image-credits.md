# Picture credits

The logo and stickers in `web/src/assets` are the bar's own pictures, partly made with Gemini and cut out for
the till. They do not fall under the licence below.

The product pictures in `server/internal/store/catalog` come from [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org),
photographed by its contributors and published under
[CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). For the till they were cut out of their
background or cropped, scaled and placed on white, and these edited versions stay under the same licence.
Each page linked below shows who took the photo. A few photos show another size of the same product, so the
barcode in the table and the code in the link can differ.

The brands, logos and pack designs in the pictures belong to their owners. They are shown only to identify
the products the bar sells.

Most products show pictures the bar collected itself: shop
and maker photos, cut to the product and put on white for the till. So do the three Pfand keys and, in
Verleih, the cue, the darts and the airhockey set (`web/src/assets/lend-*.webp`). These pictures do not fall
under the licence above; they are shown only to identify what the bar sells. None of the products on sale
uses an Open Food Facts photo any more.
Products the bar no longer sells stay in the table, because their pictures stay in the program for the day
one of them comes back.

The cannabis leaf in the 4,20 € joke (`web/src/till/cannabisLeaf.js`) is
[Cannabis icon.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Cannabis_icon.svg) by svgsilh.com from Wikimedia
Commons, dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The till adds a light vein down each finger.

The pictures for Bier 0,5 l, Bier 0,33 l, Mineralwasser 1,5 l, Andere Schokoriegel and Bechergericht are
drawn from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) icons (ISC License).
